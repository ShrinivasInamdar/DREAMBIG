An Startup - Industry - Student Connect 17th April 2026

Dream big

Join us for the fifth edition of DBCE Dream Big – Industry Startup Student Connect, an event dedicated to fostering collaboration between students, industry leaders, and startups.
Dream Big serves as a dynamic platform where students can gain first-hand industry exposure, career insights, and networking opportunities.
Designed to bridge the gap between academia and the professional world, this event enables students to interact with industry experts, explore cutting-edge technologies, and understand real-world industry trends. By engaging with renowned speakers, startup founders, and innovators, participants will gain valuable knowledge beyond textbooks, helping them make informed career choices.
Dream Big is more than just a conference—it’s an experiential learning opportunity that equips students with industry-relevant skills, entrepreneurial inspiration, and a strong professional network. Whether you're a student eager to explore career opportunities, an entrepreneur looking for fresh talent, or a faculty member seeking industry-academic collaboration,
Dream Big 2026 is the event for you.
Join us and take a step toward shaping your future!

Our Speakers

Mr. Sumukh Kamat

Founder - The Energy Footprint

ABOUT

Founder of The Energy Footprint, is a design thinker and transformation leader focused on building sustainable, high-performing systems. With a strong emphasis on circular economy and zero-waste practices, he works with organizations to move beyond incremental improvements and create meaningful, long-term impact. With experience across energy efficiency, innovation, and project management, he focuses on creating measurable outcomes by strengthening fundamentals and enabling people to perform at their best, combining process excellence, mentorship, behavioral change, and AI-driven workflows to help organizations innovate, scale, and operate more effectively.

Ms. Asha Arondekar

Entrepreneur - Global to Local

ABOUT

Entrepreneur, social activist, and motivational speaker dedicated to empowering communities and driving meaningful change. As Chair Person G100 and State President Goa for ABWCI, she plays a key role in fostering leadership and supporting women in business and industry. With experience as CEO of Global to Local Goa and leadership roles in organizations like the Goa Chamber of Commerce and Industries, she focuses on promoting local entrepreneurship, uplifting women, and creating platforms that enable growth, collaboration, and sustainable impact.
Expand

Mr. Ankur Kankonkar

Founder - IncluSeer

ABOUT

Founder of IncluSeer, is a blind technologist and accessibility leader focused on building inclusive, scalable solutions for enterprises. With a strong emphasis on accessibility intelligence, he works on measuring, benchmarking, and governing accessibility risk at an enterprise level. As CEO of IncluSeer and AnklyticX, he brings strategic leadership in aligning business goals with inclusive innovation, helping organizations integrate accessibility into their core systems while driving meaningful impact through technology and data-driven decision-making.
